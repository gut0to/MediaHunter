# Code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Welcome different backgrounds and experience
levels, give specific and constructive feedback, and obtain consent before
sharing anyone's personal information. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
unwanted sexual attention, and retaliation are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect contributors.
Report conduct privately to a maintainer through a contact method listed on
their GitHub profile, or use GitHub's report feature for abusive accounts or
content. Do not post sensitive details publicly.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
